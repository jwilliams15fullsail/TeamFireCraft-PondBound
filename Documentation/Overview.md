# Pond Bound

### Overview/Executive Summary
#### ELEVATOR PITCH / HIGH CONCEPT
“Pond Bound” is a 1–4 player cooperative physics-driven adventure where players try to escape an 
experimental research facility and make their way back to their home pond using charge-based 
jumping, tongues, and teamwork to traverse oversized environments while managing hydration, 
hunger, and random events. Whether playing alone or with friends, there should be chaotic and 
comedic moments unique to each adventure.

#### GENRE
1st/3rd Person Adventure

#### DESIGN PILLARS
•	Thrill/Exhilaration
•	Tenacity
•	Curiosity

#### KEY/EXOTIC FEATURES
•	“Little Frog Big World” – Large Traversable Environments
Taking advantage of the players’ stature as a frog and physics-driven interactions, 
we can turn a simple room into a massive playground. With a variety of environments to 
traverse players will have more events to interact with and must think creatively to progress.

•	Frog Movement
The primary methods for overcoming environmental obstacles will be a charge-based 
jumping mechanic and a multipurpose tongue that can grab/interact with enemies, allies, 
and objects in the environment. Players could use their tongue to save an ally after 
a poorly executed jump, swing across gaps, and catch dinner throughout the adventure.

#### CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP
Explore Environment -> Traverse Obstacles Using Frog Movement -> Encounter Enemies/Events -> 
Discover Powerups -> Manage Health/Stamina/Hydration -> Reach Checkpoint? -> Repeat (until the goal)

#### WIN CONDITION / PLAYER GOAL
Player reaches the pond -> Win
You’ve made it back home and you will be rewarded with a happy splash art of the player reaching 
the pond, an adventure report summing up the events of your playthrough, and fun unlockable cosmetics.

#### LOSS CONDITION / CONTINUATION
All players HP drop to zero -> Run is over? / Reset to checkpoint if team lives remain?
Sadly, you didn’t reach home… You will be rewarded with a sad splash art of the player/players 
facing their doom in whichever environment they were traversing, an adventure report, and any unlockables.
